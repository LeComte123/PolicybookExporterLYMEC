(Passed Autumn 2024\)

Considering that:

* Article 26 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights ensures that everyone has the right to education and that education shall be directed to the full development of the human personality and the strengthening of respect for human rights and fundamental freedom

* The International Court of Justice determined in 2007 that the acts committed in Srebrenica during the Bosnian war constituted acts of genocide.

* The International Criminal Tribunal for the former Yugoslavia (ICTY) has found former leaders of Bosnian Serbs Radovan Karadžić and Ratko Mladić guilty for their roles in genocide, ethnic cleansing and crimes against humanity and sentenced them to life imprisonment.

* The Pedagogical Institute of Republika Srpska entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina has revised the primary school history curriculum which now presents Radovan Karadžić and Ratko Mladić as historical figures who held important military and political positions rather than war criminals.

* The denial of genocide, as an attempt to deny or minimize the scale and severity of an instance of genocide, is an integral part of genocide

Noting that:

* The Resolution 2574 (2024) of the Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe on Bosnia and Herzegovina encourages the establishment of a common core curriculum in history allowing for all to understand the diversity of points of view while prohibiting the honoring in the school curriculums of individuals convicted of genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes.

* The Constitutional Court of Bosnia and Herzegovina temporarily suspended a part of the primary school curriculum in Republika Srpska entity of Bosnia and

Herzegovina presenting individuals convicted of war criminals as historic figures.

* The OSCE mission in Bosnia and Herzegovina has criticized these changes in curriculum stating that they threaten critical reforms in history teaching, perpetuate divisions, and obscure the complex roots of the violence.

Recognizing that:

* The educational systems should be unbiased and should not deepen the divisions, teach intolerance, and promote genocide denialism or glorification of convicted war criminals.

* The true reconciliation and long-lasting peace in the Western Balkans can be achieved only if the truth about the atrocities and war crimes is taught in an objective and unbiased way

* The European Union has a role in promoting peace, ensuring tolerance, and protecting human rights.

* Therefore, LYMEC calls for:

* Condemnation of the new school curriculums in Republika Srpska entity of Bosnia and Herzegovina which honor individuals convicted of genocide, crimes against humanity, and war crimes.

* Developing appropriate educational programs in the European Union that would ensure teaching about the Srebrenica genocide and prevent future instances of genocide glorification or denial.

* Introducing stricter standards towards aspiring European Union member countries that would ensure that their school curriculums, textbooks, and educational materials do not honor, celebrate, or glorify sentenced war criminals.
