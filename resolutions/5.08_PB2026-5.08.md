(FY), Liberal Democratic League of Ukraine (LDLU), YGL, SVB, Liberal Youth of Sweden (LUF), Norges Unge Venstre (NUV), Attīstībai Youth (AY), Young Progressive Slovakia (MPS), ERPY, Youth Forum of Naša Stranka (YFNS), Lithuanian Liberal Youth (LLY), Radikale Ungdom (RU), Venstres Ungdom (VU), Centerstudenter (CS), Young Liberals (YL), Jonge Democraten NL (JD), Jongliberalen (JL), Bundesverband Liberaler Hochschulgruppen (LHG), JUNOS \- Junge Liberale Neos, JNC*  
*Considering that:*

* The Hungarian Parliament passed a new legislation on the 18th of March, 2025, that prohibits those assemblies which “violate the children’s right to healthy development”.

* This new legislation is intended to ban the Budapest Pride march.

* According to the new legislation, not just the organizers, but also the participants of such assemblies can be fined by the police up to €500. If the fine is not paid in due time, it will be collected as tax.  
* To identify those who participate in illegal assemblies the new legislation enables the police to deploy facial-recognition software, which uses artificial intelligence to automatically assign identity to each face that is captured on CCTV footage.  
* The European Union’s AI Act, which was passed by the European Parliament in 2023, has forbidden the use of AI-based facial recognition systems against civil targets. According to the AI Act, such softwares can be used only against perpetrators of serious crimes, such as terrorism, and in order to find missing persons. However, these exemptions always require permission from judicial authorities.  
* Six Members of the Hungarian Parliament protested with smoke candles and flyers during the voting of the “Lex-PRIDE” law. The Speaker of the Hungarian Parliament, László Kövér, imposed a total of 82 million HUF (€205,000) record fine for those six representatives and banned them from the House for several sessions.

*Believing that:*

* The PRIDE march poses absolutely no threat to children and it doesn’t violate any of the children’s rights.

* Every person has the right to assemble peacefully.

* The use of facial recognition software to identify and to fine Budapest Pride participants breaches the European Union’s AI Act.

* Modern AI-driven facial recognition softwares should not be used against those who gather peacefully.

* The Presiding Officer of any national parliament should not ban democratically elected representatives arbitrarily.

*LYMEC calls for:*

* The ALDE party, Renew Europe and the European Commission to publish a statement of solidarity with the LGBTQ+ community of Hungary.

* The European Commission to launch an infringement procedure against the Government of Hungary for applying the “Lex-PRIDE” law.

* The ALDE party and Renew Europe to publish a statement of solidarity with those six Members of the Hungarian Parliament who have been fined and banned from the House by the Speaker for the protest against the “Lex-PRIDE” law.  
* The ALDE party, Renew Europe and the European Commission to continue raising awareness of the Orbán-government’s attacks against the Hungarian freedom of press and freedom of speech.
