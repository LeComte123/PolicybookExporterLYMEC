(Passed Autumn 2024\)

Title: The case Moldova: Russian meddling in European democracies, time to act Noting that:

* Russia attempted to subvert the recent 2024 Moldovan referendum on European Union membership and the 2024 Moldovan presidential election;

* Russia specifically sought to influence Moldovans to vote against EU membership and the pro-European candidate, Maia Sandu, by offering payments totaling 39 million USD through an internationally sanctioned Russian bank to over 130,000 recipients, according to prosecutors;

* The outcome of the presidential election in Moldova was a clear commitment of the Moldovan people to freedom, democracy, and European integration despite Russian meddling;

* The recent re-election of Donald Trump as US president is posing a tremendous threat to the military security and stability of Europe and the world;

* Russia's influence in Europe is on the rise;

* Several political actors in Moldova disingenuously pretended to be pro-European but campaigned against the recent referendum;

* In the run-off to the second round of elections, bomb threats made it difficult for voters abroad to cast their votes;

* Volunteers got threatened while campaigning;

* Undercover investigation from the Moldovan newspaper ‘Ziarul de Gardă’ clearly unveiled deep ties between Ilan Șor and the Kremlin in their ‘În slujba Moscovei’ report.

* Russia is illegally occupying part of Moldova, and that any attempts to negotiate the withdrawal of its so-called "peace keepers" have failed because of Russia acting aggressively, illegally and in bad-faith

Pointing out that:

* There is an immediate risk of further escalation in the Russian war on Ukraine if

U.S. military aid were to end under a potential new Trump administration.;

* Moldava is one of the main targets of Russian foreign interference;

* At the same time as the first round of the presidential election, in a referendum held at the same time, the country's EU membership was enshrined in the constitution as a national objective by a narrow majority.

Believing that:

* Moldova belongs with the rest of democratic Europe and the free world, standing united in shared values and aspirations;

* Freedom, democracy, and rule of law are necessary conditions to ensure the respect of human rights;

* Election interference of all kinds—internal or external—are hindering democratic decision making processes.

LYMEC to call for:

* European Governments to support the Moldovan government in its efforts to strengthen democracy and the rule of law in the country;

* The EU to help the further integration of the Republic of Moldova into the European contractual framework;

* The EU to acknowledge the will of the Moldovan people to join the Union;

* Reducing tariffs in order to strengthen economic ties between Europe and Moldova (and therefore reducing the dependency on Russia) through trade and lifting visa restrictions for Moldovan citizens.

* European Governments to recognize and support Maia Sandu’s presidential election victory;

* Liberal political and civil society organisations in Moldova to aid the pro-European government in Chisinau and Maia Sandu in combating Russian influence and in enforcing reforms that will align the country with European standards;

* The European Union, in tandem with Moldovan authorities, to expand communication campaigns to raise public awareness of the benefits of EU integration for Moldova and to promote understanding of the challenges, including Russian interference;

* The European Union to strengthen technical and advisory assistance to help Moldova navigate accession negotiations and manage the substantial workload as Moldova works towards EU integration.

* Support for Republic of Moldova’s goal to join the EU by 2030

* The EU and its Member States to assist Moldova in creating and implementing a long-term plan for peace, stability, economic prosperity and international rules-based order in Transnistria

* ALDE Party and LYMEC MOs to help lobby for European Acquis Communautaire and provide the necessary political support for Republic of Moldova's European integration
