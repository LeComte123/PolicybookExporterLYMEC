Party Youth, Junge Liberale, Centre Party Youth, For Youth\! (Latvia), Mladi Pokreta slobodnih građana, Bundesverband Liberaler Hochschulgruppen, Liberal Democratic

League of Ukraine, Alliance Youth Considering that:

* War technology is rapidly evolving and changing the present warfare doctrine,

  evidenced by the increasing use and development of unmanned drones, small-scale

  terrorism, and diverse hybrid attack tactics;

* Russian hybrid attacks on the European Union (EU) and its infrastructure, including cyberattacks on various national agencies, Russia’s shadow fleet and

  energy disruptions in the Baltic Sea, and especially recent drone sightings, cause widespread panic and undermine trust in the ability of states and institutions such as the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO) and the EU to

  ensure national security;

* Shooting down drones in densely populated areas poses significant risks, including the potential damage to civilian infrastructure and threats people’s  
  lives and health;

* Since the beginning of 2025, Russia has launched several drones targeting EU countries, including areas around Finland, Lithuania, Estonia, Denmark, France,

  Belgium, Germany, Poland, and Romania;

* Drone sightings at several European airports, where experts speculate Russian involvement, have disrupted air traffic, caused chaos, and created hazardous

  conditions;

* Drones have also been sighted at military bases such as the Belgian base Kleine

Brogel which also stores US nuclear weapons; Noting that:

* Russia’s hybrid attacks on the EU have nearly doubled in frequency since its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, with 86% of the 2,019 recorded incidents  
  occurring after 2022;  
* Some drone sightings, in the event of an explosion, could cause injuries or fatalities within a 50-meter radius;

* Recent Russian drone sightings clearly test and undermine NATO’s responsiveness

  to hybrid actions, especially given the current absence of a legal framework;

* Most EU and NATO Member States have not sufficiently invested in anti-drone defense technologies, making the cost of drone neutralization technology higher

  than the cost of producing the drones themselves;

* Some EU countries’, laws restrict security forces from shooting down unknown drones unless they are armed or pose a direct threat to critical infrastructure;

* Besides drones, unmanned Belarusian helium Balloons have causes similar issues

for Baltic states and airports; Believing that:

* All EU and NATO countries have the right to equal safety and protection;

* Russia’s hybrid warfare tactics are aimed at undermining EU democracy;

* Securing the airspace of EU and NATO Member States is becoming increasingly

  vital for maintaining our security;

* NATO’s deterrence strategy against Russia’s continued attacks on EU countries

  cannot succeed without the proper technology and tools to counter drone incursions;

* The security of the EU depends significantly on the security of Eastern European

states, especially those bordering Russia; LYMEC calls for:

* NATO to recognize the security of Eastern borders as a priority for the security of all of Europe;

* The EU to immediately increase funding for anti-drone defense systems and drone

  walls, technology capable of tracing, identifying, and seizing unauthorized drones in countries bordering Russia;

* The EU to invest and collaborate with European start-ups specializing in drone defense manufacturing;  
* European States to invest in drone detection systems, especially around critical  
  infrastructure such as airports, along with training for field professionals and eventually create an integrated EU web of anti-drone systems;

* The EU and NATO Member States to accelerate initiate the creation and implementation of new response procedures and laws to address Russian hybrid

  attacks and enable states to take down unauthorised drones entering their territory;

* European States to prioritize the procurement of affordable drones and anti-drone technologies, such as small, inexpensive missiles or low-range attack drones, that can be reused multiple times, minimizing financial loss;

* All EU countries to invest in intelligence on Russian and Belarusian military strategies and technologies, adjusting EU defense systems accordingly by employing radio detector sensors, radio frequency and physical jammers, and  
  other interception technologies;

* European states to continue increasing support for Ukraine while minimizing relations with Russia;

* Strengthen collaboration between the EU, Ukraine and partner European states,

  learning from Ukraine’s experience and its implementation of a drone wall.
