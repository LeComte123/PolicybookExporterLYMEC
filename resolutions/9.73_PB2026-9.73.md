(Passed Spring 2025\) Submitted by: CUF

Co-signers: Alliance Youth, Unge Venstre, Nowoczesna Youth, Molodezhka, Venstres Ungdom, Svensk Ungdom, ERPY, Youth Forum of Naša stranka, JeunesMR, Silvia Fernández (IMS), HSLS, Lithuanian Liberal Youth, LUF, Centerstudenter.

*Noting that:*

* In October 2024 elections were held in Georgia. Many called for a re-run due to significant irregularities, among them the EU parliament who declared the election as neither free nor fair.  
* Georgian Dream declared themselves the winner, and no new election has been held.  
* The government has delayed the country’s EU membership bid until 2028\.

* In September 2024, Georgia’s parliament recently approved new legislation decreasing LGBTQIA+ rights.  
* The bill seeks to ban rights such as same-sex marriage, adoption by same-sex couples and gender-affirming care.  
* Several European countries still list Georgia as a safe country of origin.  
* Article 8 of the European Convention on Human Rights guarantees the right to respect for private and family life, free from unnecessary state interference.

* LYMEC has previously addressed the European perspective for Georgia and called for closer cooperation with civil society in its resolution “Closing Putin's gates means welcoming allies \- Extending an invitation to Georgia”;

*Considering that:*

* An overwhelming majority (over 80%) of Georgian citizens support EU membership.  
* Pro-LGBTQIA+ organizations in Georgia, who already face difficult circumstances, have indicated that the new laws would force them to shut down.  
* Lists of safe countries of origin exist to speed up asylum applications, putting Georgian LGBTQIA+ asylum seekers at risk for deportation.

* Only a day after the so-called ‘family values’ bill was passed, one of the first openly trans public figures in Georgia, Kesaria Abramidze, was murdered in her home.

* Hundreds of people protesting the government have been arrested, and among them more than 300 have suffered torture, beatings and other ill-treatment from law enforcement (according to Amnesty International).  
* In February 2025 49 opposition MPs were expelled from Parliament by the government.  
* EU funding to Georgia has mainly focused on economic development to raise quality of life for citizens.

* This EU assistance is conditional, leading to funds being frozen or stopped due to democratic backsliding.

* According to CNN, the US’ freeze in foreign aid has damaged civil society and independent media in Georgia.

*Believing that:*

* One of the ways the EU can promote democracy and human rights in Georgia is to support the local organizations working to uphold these values.

* Independent media and civil society are two cornerstones of democracy.

* While EU funds in general should continue to be strictly conditional on democratic progress, we should do more to support independent media and civil society in Georgia directly.  
* The so-called Family Values bill will undermine the fundamental rights of the Georgian people, while increasing discrimination, stigmatization and violence against LGBTQIA+ people.

* LGBTQIA+ people are not safe to be living freely without persecution in Georgia.

* Georgia is no longer fit to be on any European country’s safe state list.

*We therefore call for:*

* LYMEC to condemn the so-called Family Values bill.

* EU member states and representatives to maintain pressure on Georgia to withdraw the legislation.  
* The EU to offer financial aid to independent media and civil society in Georgia that maintain democratic values.  
* All EU member states to immediately remove Georgia from their safe state lists.  
* All EU member states and European States to impose Visa sanctions on members of the Georgian Dream government responsible for the attack on human rights in Georgia
