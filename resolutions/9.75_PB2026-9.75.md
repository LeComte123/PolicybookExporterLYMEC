(Passed Spring 2025\)

*Considering that:*

* On 1 November 2024, the canopy of Novi Sad railway station collapsed causing the deaths of 15 people including two children  
* The railway station in Novi Sad was reconstructed by the Ministry of Construction, Transportation, and Infrastructure and opened just four months before the collapse  
* The Government of Serbia has not taken any responsibility for the tragedy and has repeatedly falsely stated that the collapsed canopy was not part of the reconstruction  
* The lack of a response from the Government and the reluctance of the Prosecutor’s office to do a full investigation in this case has caused university students to start a blockade of faculties across the country

*Noting that:*

* Around 75% of the state-owned faculties in Serbia are currently under blockade

* A significant number of high schools, some elementary schools, and preschool institutions have also followed the blockade of their activities

* The political situation in Serbia has become increasingly polarised which escalated with protests in the National Assembly and multiple city assemblies around the country  
* The current student protests have been the biggest gathering of citizens of Serbia since the 5 October 2000 protests that led to the overthrowing of the regime of Slobodan Milošević

* Student protests in Serbia have inspired similar student protests in the neighboring countries including Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Montenegro, Slovenia, and North Macedonia but also throughout the Serbian diaspora in Europe and the rest of the world

* Numerous instances of violence against peaceful protestors have been recorded including physical attacks with baseball bats, multiple car

rammings targeting protestors, and arbitrary detentions as intimidation tactics by authorities

*Recognizing that:*

* The citizen protests are the cornerstone of every functioning liberal democracy

* The freedom of assembly and peaceful protest are fundamental rights that are protected under European and international law

* The actions of the Government of Serbia contradict fundamental European values and have been undermining its path of becoming a member of the European Union

* The Prosecutor’s office still hasn’t fully investigated all of the details of the canopy collapse and indicted all of the people responsible for this tragedy

* A significant number of professional organizations, cultural institutions, athletes, and artists from Serbia and the wider region have supported the student protest

*Therefore, LYMEC calls for:*

* Supporting the right of Serbian students to protest peacefully, free from intimidation, violence, and political persecution

* ALDE Party and Renew Europe to give full support to the student protests, condemn the violence against the students, and request the respect of the rule of law in Serbia  
* The Government of Serbia to fulfill the demands of the students to the fullest including publishing all of the documentation concerning the collapse of the canopy at Novi Sad Railway Station, indicting those who are responsible for the collapse, identifying and punishing people responsible for the attacks on students and citizens who were demonstrating, dropping charges against people arrested at the protests and a greater budget allocations for education  
* The European Commission to put pressure on the Government of Serbia to fulfill all the student demands  
* The European Commission and European Council to ensure that all of the countries seeking membership in the European Union have the highest level of respect for individual freedoms and human rights, ensure full protection of the right to protest in line with EU standards, and ensure transparent, accountable governance in line with European democratic principles
