**Passed Autumn 2025**

Submitted by: Nowoczesna Youth (Poland)

Co-signed by: Project NEON (Greece), ZeMolodizhka (Ukraine), Jóvenes de Ciudadanos

(Spain), Young Green Liberals (Switzerland), Young Progressive Slovakia (Slovakia), Svensk Ungdom (Finland), Venstres Ungdom (Denmark), Jonge Democraten (Netherlands),  
Mladi Pokreta Slobodnih Građana (Serbia), Radikal Ungdom (Denmark), Attīstībai Youth  
(Latvia), Young Liberals (UK), Arthur Wu (IMS), Liberala ungdomsförbundet (LUF), Youth Forum of Naša stranka (Bosnia and Herzegovina), Junge Liberale (Germany) Joventut Nacionalista de Catalunya (Catalonia)

Considering that:

* Continuous provision of military support to Ukraine in arguably the most crucial

  moment in the war since 2022 remains to be of great importance

* Russia ceaselessly conducts hybrid attacks on European strategic and civilian infrastructure, having intensified its activity over the recent months

* The EU has been a long-standing advocate of upholding international law, democratic values and protection of human rights, which makes it crucial for

  Europe to take decisive action against Russia’s illegal and violent actions

* The EU has already demonstrated its strong commitment to solidarity with Ukraine  
  through economic sanctions and political support, making it imperative to follow  
  through these measures by addressing the financial means Russia uses to fund its

  war efforts

* US President Donald Trump acts inconsistently and hesitantly in assisting Ukraine in its defense against the Russian aggression, thus prompting increased

  necessity for action on the part of the European Union

* The seizure of Russian funds and handing them over to Ukraine would be a suitable punishment for Russia's hybrid war against Poland, Finland, the Baltic

  states and the whole European Union

* The seizure of Russian assets and handing them over to Ukraine would nevertheless pose legal and financial risks to Belgium

Believing that:

* In times of crisis, cooperation and unity of European states is key to sustaining peace and prosperity within the region

* Liberal democracies, founded upon the rule of law, must act decisively to deter

  authoritarian regimes from violating territorial integrity and international law, as failing to do so would erode the values that bind Europe together

* The European states should build up Ukraine’s military capabilities in order to deter Russia and prevent a war at a larger scale  
* Vladimir Putin is a war criminal, who does not honor his commitments, and is therefore an unreliable business partner

* As a member of the European Community, Belgium should not be left alone in the

  face of potential legal threats from Russia, and it is imperative that the EU provides a robust and united legal framework to ensure the protection of Belgium’s sovereignty and legal position within this matter

* Establishing a transparent and unified EU procedure for asset seizure will strengthen the rule of law and demonstrate Europe’s determination to hold

Russia

accountable, preventing future instances of authoritarian overreach Welcoming:

* Polish FM Radosław Sikorski’s statement about Polish readiness to provide financial aid in case of legal filings against Belgium made by Russian Federation and his eagerness to push this motion forward within the European

  Union

* German Chancellor Friedrich Merz’s forthright tone when discussing the matter.

  We perceive his stance as a positive gradual shift from Berlin’s previous cautious position

* Danish PM Mette Frederiksen’s support of the idea

* Swedish PM Ulf Kristersson’s favourability towards the notion discussed

* The arrest warrant on Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin issued by the International Criminal Court and its calls for the accountability of individuals responsible for Russia’s war crimes, signaling a unified global push for justice

* The support of the European Commission for the exploration of broader frameworks

  for compensating Ukraine and other victims of Russian aggression, that would

  further solidify Europe’s commitment to holding Russia accountable for its acts

of war LYMEC Calls For:

* The seizure of Russian assets valued at €140 billion held under the supervision of Belgian financial institution Euroclear

* Handing the funds over to Ukraine in order to reinforce its defensive effort against Russian unlawful aggression upon its territorial integrity and the human  
  rights of its citizens

* Providing Belgium with full legal protection in the process of seizing the funds and over the course of potential legal trials in case of a lawsuit filed by the Russian Federation

* Providing aid and ensuring the financial security to the Kingdom of Belgium in the event of an unfavourable verdict of the court

* An EU-wide declaration of support for Belgium, as well as the European Union’s

  unified assistance over the course of the seizure of Russian assets and their provision to appropriate bodies in Ukraine
