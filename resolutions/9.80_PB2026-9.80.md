**Noting that:**

* According to United Nations OCHA and WHO reports (as of September 2025), more

  than 67,000 Palestinians (of which more than 20,000 were children) have been

  killed since the Israeli Government launched its assault on Gaza, following Hamas’ brutal terrorist attack on 7th October 2023;

* Hamas’ brutal terrorist attack on 7th October 2023 resulted in the killing of at least 1,219 Israeli civilians, and the taking of 251 hostages, with widespread evidence of the use of torture and sexual violence;  
* Since 7th October 2023, more than 169,000 Palestinians have been injured, and  
  over 80% of Gaza’s population – approximately 1.8 million people – have been

  forcibly displaced;

* Since 7th October 2023, Hamas has increased its totalitarian rule over Gaza, torturing and executing Gazan opposition forces, displaying its disinterest in being a political force representing the interests of the Gazan and Palestinian

  population;

* Over 90% of Palestinians living in Gaza are living within a crisis level of malnutrition; reports that much of Gaza’s water extraction, desalination and sewage management facilities have been destroyed;

* At least 94% of hospitals in Gaza have been damaged or destroyed since October  
  2023, and many are no longer operational;

* Much of the West Bank still remains under Israeli military control and the

  Israeli government has enabled 160 settlements to be built in the West Bank and

  East Jerusalem since 1967;

* Israel and its civilian population had to endure constant attacks by terrorist groups such as Hamas, Palestinian Islamic Jihad, Hezbollah and the Houthis,

as

well as from the Islamic Republic of Iran itself. The mentioned terrorist groups are used as proxies by the regime of the Islamic Republic of Iran;

* There have been strong efforts by the parliamentary opposition parties in Israel

  - led by Yesh Atid \- to force the Netanjahu government to end the war in Gaza

  and bring all hostages held in Gaza back to Israel;

* There has been massive opposition in Israel against the war in Gaza, as displayed by masses of people participating in protests throughout Israel \- led

  by the families of the hostages \- calling on the Netanjahu government to end the

  war and bring all hostages held in Gaza back to Israel;

* The recent report presented to the Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, Including East Jerusalem, and Israel on 16th September 2025 by the UN Human Rights Council, which concluded  
  that “\[genocidal intent\] is the only inference that could reasonably be drawn  
  from the pattern of conduct” displayed by the Israeli security forces towards Palestinians in Gaza;  
* Additional reports from Human Rights Watch, the International Association of Genocide Scholars and Amnesty UK concur with the findings of the UN report that  
  the actions of Israeli security forces meet the legal definition of genocide in Article II of the United Nations Convention for the Prevention and Punishment of  
  the Crime of Genocide (1948);

* Much of the West Bank still remains under Israeli military control and the

  Israeli government has enabled 160 settlements to be built in the West Bank and

  East Jerusalem since 1967;

* Israel, its civilian population, and Druze minorities in neighbouring states have had to endure constant attacks by various terrorist groups, as well as from  
  the Islamic Republic of Iran. Many of these terrorist groups, including Hamas, have been used as proxies by the Iranian regime.

* There have been strong efforts by the parliamentary opposition parties in Israel

  - led by Yesh Atid and Young Yesh Atid \- to force the Netanjahu government to

  end the war in Gaza and bring all hostages held in Gaza back to Israel;

* There has been massive opposition in Israel against the war in Gaza and the use

  of military force against civilians, as displayed by masses of people participating in protests throughout Israel \- led by the families of the hostages \- calling on the Netanjahu government to end the war, stop the bombardment of civilian infrastructure, and bring all hostages held in Gaza back

  to Israel;

* On 9th October 2025, the Israeli Government and Hamas signed a peace plan

  facilitated by the US Government which established a ceasefire, and enabled the

  return of the 20 remaining living as well as all dead hostages held and killed by Hamas and the release of over 2,000 Palestinian citizens held by the Israeli

  Government.

**Understanding that:**

* Extreme and indiscriminate uses of violence – by both state and non-state actors

  – represent flagrant violations of international humanitarian law and the Geneva

  Conventions;

* The principles of international law must be upheld, as enshrined in the UN Charter, the Rome Statute of the International Criminal Court (ICC), and the Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide (1948) as

  well as the justice convention’s ban on genocide;

* The International Criminal Court has the authority to exercise its jurisdiction over alleged genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes, and the crime of

  aggression committed on the territory of Palestine, as well as over such crimes

  committed by nationals of Palestine irrespective of where they occur, and acknowledging that its determinations of individual criminal responsibility are

  binding, LYMEC commits to respecting the Court’s independent role and outcomes.

* The International Court of Justice has the authority to adjudicate state responsibility under the Genocide Convention and to interpret and apply the  
  Convention in light of the peremptory norm prohibiting genocide, LYMEC commits  
  to respecting its rulings in South Africa v Israel and in any related proceedings.

* The Two-State Solution is widely regarded as the most likely route to securing a durable, sustainable, and secure peace for the people of Israel and Palestine;

* A Two-State Solution is only possible when political groups in Israel and Palestine accept a mutual right for both states to co-exist and when terrorist groups that have called for the extermination of Israel (including Hamas and

  Palestinian Islamic Jihad), as well as armed settler groups in the West Bank, are dissolved and disarmed;

* In September 2025, several European states formally recognised the State of Palestine as part of their commitment to supporting a durable peace plan;

* The universality of human rights and the indivisibility of freedom are inherent values supported by young Europeans committed to liberal democracy, human  
  rights, and the rule of international law.

* A State of Palestine can only come into existence when it is based on democratic, transparent Palestinian institutions—respecting pluralism, the rule  
  of law, and anti-corruption safeguards, which safeguard the life of Palestinians

  and Israelis alike.

**Believing that:**

* No political objective can justify the deliberate targeting or indiscriminate killing of civilians, the taking of civilian hostages, or the deprivation of essential means of survival such as food or water;

* The right to self-determination is a core tenet of liberalism and should be respected by all state and non-state actors;  
* Peace cannot be genuine or lasting without justice, accountability, and the condemnation of crimes against humanity;  
* A true durable and sustainable peace can only emerge when cycles of violence,

  occupation, insecurity, and impunity end;

* The continuation of the conflict under the leadership of the terrorist group Hamas in Gaza and Benjamin Netanyahu’s far-right coalition in Israel has had terrible consequences for both the people of Gaza and Israel;

* Under Benjamin Netanyahu, democracy in Israel has entered a period of authoritarian backsliding which poses great risk to the freedoms, rights, and security of both Jewish and Arab citizens of Israel;

* The continuation of the conflict has enabled Hamas’ totalitarian terrorist regime to perpetuate their control over Palestinian society and life in Gaza;

* Hamas and other terrorist groups must be dissolved, disarmed and must have no

  place in the political landscape of Palestine;

* The Gaza Peace Plan must provide the basis for further diplomatic intervention

  to sustain the ceasefire, enable the end to violence, and facilitate reconciliation between the people and states of Israel and Palestine.

**LYMEC Calls For:**

* The post-war reconstruction of Gaza to be Palestinian-led and guided by a peaceful and mutually agreed framework that ensures lasting stability and co-  
  operation.

* The EU and European States to call for the dissolution and disarmament of Hamas

  and all Palestinian terrorist groups that have destabilized the region and present a threat to future peaceful relations between the Israeli and Palestinian people.

* The International Criminal Court (ICC) to proceed with investigations into war crimes and crimes against humanity that may have been committed in Israel and

  Palestine by state officials, military leaders, or members of terrorist groups, without political obstruction or selective justice.

* The European Commission to continue its efforts to facilitate the entry of humanitarian aid into Gaza, with guarantees to ensure the effective and orderly

  distribution of aid to civilians

* European states to support provisions for the establishment of a fully independent, democratic, and globally recognised Palestinian state, along mutually negotiated borders confirmed through a democratic vote, that ensures

  mutual and peaceful co-existence with the state of Israel.

* European states and the EU to call on all states that have not yet recognized the State of Israel to do so and establish full diplomatic relations.

* The establishment of a UN-led peace conference aimed at a permanent political

  settlement, involving the European Union and the United Kingdom as key facilitators, which seeks to secure international guarantees for security and self-determination of both Israelis and Palestinians.

* The continued planning and eventual implementation of IMEC as a facilitator for

  peace between Israel and the Arab world through connectivity, trade and prosperity.

* The LYMEC Bureau to take note of the findings of the UN Independent International Commission of Inquiry on the Occupied Palestinian Territory, dated

  16 September 2025, which according to the Article 255 concludes that the far-

  right Netanyahu coalition bears responsibility for the failure to prevent genocide, the commission of genocide and the failure to punish genocide against

  the Palestinians in the Gaza Strip;

* The LYMEC and ALDE bureaus to issue statements following the final ruling of the  
  International Court of Justice in the case of South Africa v. Israel, recognising the outcome and its implications for the protection of civilians, human rights, and the integrity of international law.
