Co signed by : Youth Forum of Naša stranka, Nowoczesna Youth, Adam Volf (IMS), Young Progressive Slovakia, ZeMolodizhka, Liberal Democratic Youth (LiDEM)

Objective : Strengthening the cybersecurity and intelligence capabilities of EU member states through artificial intelligence.

*Considering that :*

* Cyber threats and intelligence challenges are constantly evolving, requiring advanced

* technological capabilities.

* Artificial intelligence (AI) plays a key role in threat analysis, cyberattack detection, and enhancing intelligence capabilities.

* The European Union's (EU) technological dependence on non-European actors, particularly the United States and China, constitutes a strategic and sovereignty issue.

* The EU has an ecosystem of startups and companies specializing in AI that should be supported and integrated into the development of cybersecurity and intelligence solutions.

*Noting that :*

* The European Union's digital infrastructure has been increasingly targeted by state-sponsored cyberattacks, notably originating from Russia and China, with over 2.2 billion records breached across 556 publicly disclosed incidents in Europe in 2024 alone.  
* Actors, particularly Russia, China, North Korea and Iran highlighting the vulnerability of its digital infrastructure.

* In 2024, hackers affiliated with these states targeted European institutions, including the UK Ministry of Defense and European parliamentarians, compromising sensitive data.

* Already in 2022, a Russian cyberattack against the Viasat satellite network disrupted communications in Europe, illustrating the strategic use of cyberspace.  
* In response to these growing threats, the EU Council has identified key risks, including attacks on system availability, ransomware, and data threats.  
* European companies are developing innovative technologies that can contribute to the EU's strategic independence.

*Believing that :*

* Given the increasing cyberattacks and geopolitical tensions, such as the war in Ukraine, Europe must be united and efficient in its cybersecurity efforts to respond swiftly and protect its digital sovereignty and strategic interests.  
* By fostering independent expertise on cybersecurity, the EU can better protect its digital and strategic assets from external threats, reduce vulnerabilities, and ensure that its security measures align with European values and priorities, without relying on foreign influence or technologies.

* Supporting European AI companies strengthens innovation within the EU, enhances local job creation, and safeguards against potential geopolitical risks tied to foreign-controlled technologies. Reducing dependence on the

  U.S. and other powers helps the EU maintain its sovereignty and control over critical technologies.

*Lymec calls for :*

* The development of a European cyber-intelligence training program for the armed forces, intelligence services, and other relevant government institutions of EU member states, in partnership with European AI-specialized organizations.  
* The deployment of a European roadmap for AI in defense, establishing a precise timeline with measurable objectives for the progressive integration of AI into cybersecurity and intelligence.

* Encouraging competitive calls for projects to attract top European companies and strengthen local innovation.

* The establishment of a public-private cooperation framework enabling European institutions and national governments to benefit from AI advancements without compromising the security and confidentiality of sensitive data.
