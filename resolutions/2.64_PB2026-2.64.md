Liberals, Young Progressive Slovakia Considering that:

* In times of heated political debate and increased polarisation, the pressure on

  civil society increased both by the means of extremist rhetoric and accusations

of “national betrayal” or propaganda, as well as by the start reduction in foreign aid.

* Beyond the EU budget, international public funding for democracy support has

  been reduced, including major cuts in USAID, the FCDO and Germany's BMZ aid

  streams, while national civic funding in Europe has declined as governments

prioritise defence and security spending, particularly within NATO member states.

Noting that:

* Eurosceptics and far-right Members of the European Parliament have launched  
  attacks on civil society and NGOs under the false pretense of demanding higher  
  scrutiny for EU spending.  
* In several Member States, such as Hungary and Slovakia, political leaders have  
  sought to discredit civil society, labelling it as “foreign-funded” or “propagandistic”, thereby undermining democracy and the rule of law.  
* Europe has witnessed a growing crackdown on activists, with instances of the excessive use of force by police against those who exercise their right to peaceful protest, as well as harassment and physical threats targeting human-  
  rights defenders and civil-society activists.

Believing that:

* A strong and independent civil society is indispensable to a liberal and democratic Europe, ensuring accountability, pluralism, and protection of fundamental rights within the European Union.

* Human rights and equality are not “niche” priorities or trading tokens in the EU budget negotiations, but the foundation of the European project, as enshrined in  
  Article 2 of the Treaty on European Union.

* Transparent, stable and directly managed EU funding mechanisms are the best

  safeguards against political interference or capture by illiberal governments.

* The Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values (CERV) programme has provided essential lifelines for organisations defending the rights of minorities and marginalised groups, including Muslims, Jewish and Roma communities, LGBTQIA+

people, migrants and refugees.

LYMEC therefore resolves to:

* Welcome the European Commission’s current commitment to increase the overall

  budget for European values, democracy, fundamental rights and freedoms through

the newly established AgoraEU programme.

* Call on the European Commission and Member States to ensure that AgoraEU funding

  retains all key topics and objectives of the ongoing CERV programme (promoting

  equality and minority rights, improving citizen engagement and participation in

  democratic life, preventing gender-based violence and violence against under

  vulnerable groups, protect and promote Union Values), as well as to remain focused on defending democracy, individual rights, equality and media freedom,

without political interference.

* Recommend that the Commission establish a Civil Society Contact Point within

  AgoraEU to ensure inclusive access for grassroots organisations across Europe.

* Urge Member States to complement EU funds by maintaining or increasing national  
  civic funding, and to strictly refrain from using “foreign agent” laws or administrative barriers that restrict NGO work.  
* Urge that funding decisions remain transparent, depoliticised, and accessible to  
  smaller and youth-led organisations through simplified application

processes and

stable operating funding and grant framework agreements.

* Advocate for EU diplomatic and budgetary leadership to counter the global decline in democracy assistance by restoring partnerships with the remaining

structures for U.S. foreign aid, as well as deepening ties with the FCDO and other liberal allies to strengthen transatlantic support for civic space.

* Defend civic space by ensuring that no EU funds are used to restrict NGOs, journalists, or activists, but rather to empower their independence.

* Promote independent media as a cornerstone of open societies and encourage  
  targeted support to strengthen media pluralism and protect citizens from disinformation.
