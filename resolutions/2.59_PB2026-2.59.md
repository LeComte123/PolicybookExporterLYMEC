**Noting that:**

* 2025 marks the 30th anniversary of the Dayton Accords, which brought peace to Bosnia and Herzegovina, whilst 2023 marked the 25th anniversary of the Good Friday Agreement which brought an end to The Troubles in Northern Ireland  
* The history of peacebuilding in Europe is long and varied and the EU has played a crucial role within peace processes across Europe  
* The EU and the Council of Europe have served as a forum for peacebuilding by establishing and deepening diplomatic relations between states  
* The European Peace Facility was established by the EU in 2021 to prevent conflicts, ‘build and preserve peace’, and strengthen international security and represents a financial commitment of EUR 17 Billion to peace and security in Europe  
* States outside of Europe, such as the USA, have also played a crucial role in enabling and supporting European peacebuilding efforts, particularly through funding from USAID

* Many post-conflict societies have experienced significant violence, major atrocities, and periods of targeted discrimination that hinders trust between communities  
* Grassroots peacebuilding efforts play a crucial role in fostering a strong civil society in post-conflict states and enabling reconciliation.  
* Youth peacebuilders often play an essential role in grassroots peacebuilding, both as participants and leaders.

* Young people in post-conflict societies are increasingly growing up not knowing the history of peace and conflict in their region.

**Further Notes:**

* Ongoing and historical peace processes are facing various political challenges, not least as a result of foreign interference and deliberate disinformation campaigns

* Conflict deprivation can have a lasting impact on post-conflict societies, especially minority communities and groups, and often poses a key obstacle to the success of peace processes and reconciliation.

* The Trump Administration’s cuts to USAID pose a threat to international development and partnership funding which supports peacebuilding efforts within Europe

**Believing that:**

* The continued success of peacebuilding is essential for both Europe’s internal security and enabling the EU’s further enlargement  
* The attitude of the current US administration threatens to undermine or derail existing and future peace processes  
* The removal of USAID funding will leave a critical gap in support for post-conflict societies in Europe, especially those in the Balkan region.  
* The process of building sustainable peace also leads to greater tolerance and respect within society, including to minority populations

* Ensuring trust in democratic institutions and the wider peace process is essential for peacebuilding efforts

* The threat of disinformation poses an existential challenge to peacebuilding within Europe as foreign actors seek to destabilise peace process for geopolitical gain

* That young people have the potential to make a unique and valuable contribution to peacebuilding efforts across Europe

**LYMEC Calls For:**

* The EU Commission to commit to supporting existing peace processes on the European continent, in the event that the US withdraws from its historical roles as a mediator and guarantor of peace within multiple peace processes.  
* The financial and organisational expansion of the European Peace Facility and the addition of further financing pillars focused on supporting conflict resolution and enabling peacebuilding at all levels, including initiatives supporting youth peacebuilding

* The EU Commission to encourage and incentivise private investment in post-conflict regions of Europe and support their economic development.

* A Forum of European Peacebuilders to be established under the Council of Europe to encourage discussion amongst peacebuilders from across Europe.

* Support for educational programs across Europe which include topics on peace processes and the history of the European Peace Project to ensure that no generation forgets the violence of the past and the peacebuilding efforts undertaken by previous generations.

* Continued support to address novel security challenges, including disinformation and adversarial action aiming to counter peacebuilding efforts, and advance democratisation across Europe.
