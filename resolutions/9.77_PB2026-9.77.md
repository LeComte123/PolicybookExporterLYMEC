(Passed Spring 2025\)

*Considering that:*

* On 16 March 2025, there was a deadly fire in the town of Kočani in the nightclub „Pulse“ causing 59 deaths and over 180 injured victims. This was the deadliest fire incident in the history of North Macedonia.

* The nightclub, which was formerly used as a carpet warehouse, was unlicensed and broke numerous safety standards: it lacked sprinklers, sufficient emergency exits, and it only had a single fire extinguisher, amongst other things

* Widespread corruption was one of the major contributing factors to this disaster since this led to a lack of public oversight and proper safety inspections, which could have prevented this kind of disaster.  
* The lack of accountability and possible pressure on the state public prosecutor to lead the investigation in favor of the current Government has caused people across the country to protest and go on marches, the biggest of which was organized by the movement „Koj e sleden?“ (Who is next?)

*Noting that:*

* According to the Government of North Macedonia, out of 50 establishments of this nature in the whole country, only 22 have the legal licenses to work.

* The political situation in North Macedonia has become increasingly polarized, with citizens demanding to reopen investigations into incidents of the same nature such as the fire incident in the modular COVID-19 hospital in Tetovo that caused 14 deaths and the „Besa Trans“ bus fire incident in Bulgaria that caused 45 deaths.  
* Citizen movements in North Macedonia have gathered support in the

* countries in the region ,including Serbia, Greece, and Slovenia but also throughout the Macedonian diaspora in Europe and the rest of the world.

* There have been numerous instances of politically motivated hate speech and spread of misinformation, allegedly caused by different political actors with a goal of discrediting the citizen movement as not being legitimate nor independent from any political influence.

*Recognizing that:*

* Citizen protests are the cornerstone of every functioning liberal democracy

* The freedom of assembly and peaceful protest are fundamental rights that are protected under European and international law

* The actions of the Government of North Macedonia contradict fundamental European values and have been undermining its path of becoming a member of the European Union  
* A significant number of professional organizations, cultural institutions, athletes, and artists from North Macedonia and the wider region have supported the protests and marches.

*Therefore, LYMEC calls for:*

* Supporting the right of Macedonian people to protest peacefully, free from intimidation and political pressure

* The Government of North Macedonia to fulfill the demands of the protesters, including:

  * Transparently publish all documentation related to the operation of the "Pulse" club from its opening until the accident from different government and local official institutions.

  * Disciplinary, criminal, and/or political liability of all institutions that are found to have failed in their work related to the incident

  * The Independent Catering Association should start working on improving the services and operating conditions of catering facilities, and not just for deregulation and financial assistance from the state.

* Including those, in an official statement put out by the Liberal Democratic Youth (LiDeM) in North Macedonia also calls demands for:

  * A national anti-corruption initiative focusing on local governance, ensuring that municipal authorities are held responsible for the enforcement of safety laws.

  * Creation of a "Park of Corruption" on the site of the nightclub—an enduring reminder of the price we pay when corruption and negligence go unchecked. This will not only serve as a memorial but as a call to action, urging future generations to fight against a culture that prioritizes profit over human lives.

* The European Commission is to put pressure on the Government of North Macedonia to fulfill all the people’s demands

* The European Commission and European Council to ensure that all of the countries seeking membership in the European Union have the highest level of respect for individual freedoms and human rights, ensure full protection of the right to protest in line with EU standards, and ensure transparent, accountable governance in line with European democratic principles.
