(Passed Autumn 2024\)

Mover: Jonge Democraten (Netherlands)

Co-signatories: Alliance Youth, Radikal Ungdom, Young Liberals UK, Centerstudenter, Young Greenliberals (CH), Jongliberalen, LUF, CUF, USR Tineret

Considering that:

* Donald Trump has decisively won the US presidential election and the Republican Party has won a majority in both chambers of Congress;

* Donald Trump’s election is expected to have massive consequences for US policy on a wide range of topics that affect the EU and its European allies, such as support for Ukraine & NATO, trade, climate and the protection of democracy, rule of law, and human rights;

* Trump stated he would end the war in Ukraine in 24 hours, raising fears he will pressure Ukraine into unfavourable negotiations with Russia, the country that launched a full-scale, illegal and unjustified war of aggression against it;

* In terms of the US’ economic relationship with the EU, Trump is likely to follow a highly protectionist path, leading to damaging trade frictions between Europe and the US, the world's largest economic relationship;

* Trump indicated that following these elections people ‘wouldn't have to worry about voting again’, indicating his commitment to anti-democratic ideas and the risk that he will place himself above the US constitution, like he tried to do on January 6th 2021, undermining the fundamental principles of democracy;

* Trump withdrew from the Paris agreement in his first term, and is likely to not cooperate on finding sustainable solutions related to the climate crisis, jeopardising the EU's efforts to address this global challenge;

* Trump's ideas are unlikely to be tempered by any advisors willing to contradict him, as his cabinet is expected to be selected based on their loyalty towards

Trump, as indicated by a senior official on Trump’s campaign, leading to a lack of accountability and expertise in US policy-making;

* there is a real risk of the US drastically scaling back its role in European security.

* Currently, European companies in certain sectors are very dependent on US technologies and development, for example in the aerospace and telecommunications sector, where Elon Musk’s SpaceX currently holds a near monopoly for rocket launch technology within the western sphere.

* Elon Musk has played a large part in Donald Trumps presidential election campaign. Donald Trump has even gone as far as offering Elon Musk a potential spot in his administration. Regardless of this, SpaceX has received at least 15.4 billion USD from the US Government in contracts in the last decade. Under a new Trump administration, this number will probably keep going up, thus giving SpaceX an even stronger position in the spacecraft market of the west.

Believing that:

* the EU and its European allies have become dependent across many areas on US action, and won’t be able to rely on US support going forward;

* the EU must take rapid action to ensure its own strategic autonomy and defend its fundamental values and interests;

* the Trump presidency poses a threat to the liberal, rules-based order and the EU needs to take rapid action to protect its values and principles, such as democracy, human rights, and the rule of law;

* liberal youth in Europe need to take a proactive role in ensuring the EU is ready to deal with the effects of a Trump presidency, by advocating for a strong, united EU response that is in line with EU values;

* the EU and its European allies can no longer rely on US support in addressing global challenges and must take concrete steps to ensure its strategic autonomy, even if this means an increase in spending to ensure the defence of the EU’s fundamental values and interests.

* Cybersecurity threats, terrorism threats and information warfare present significant risks to the EU’s security and democratic processes, especially if U.S. intelligence cooperation and support for European cybersecurity decrease under the new administration.

* The EU and the USA share common values \- liberty, rule of law and democracy \- which bind us together in a world threatened by tyranny and which transcend whoever is sitting in the White House or Le Berlaymont.

Calls for:

* a strong, united response from the EU, hand in hand with its European allies, to the challenges posed by a Trump presidency, in defence of the EU’s shared fundamental values and the liberal, rules-based order, including a clear commitment to democracy, human rights, and the rule of law;

* the European Commission to rapidly present its impact assessment of the Trump  
  presidency across all policy files from the perspective of strategic autonomy, and to ensure the Commission is in the position to respond within the first 100 days of its mandate;

* EU and national leaders to establish a functioning working relationship with the incoming US administration to the extent possible, grounded on liberal values and the rules-based international order;

* the European Commission to prepare a strategy for safeguarding European industry

  and business in the event of a protectionist trade war launched by the incoming

  US Administration;

* an urgent increase in European defence spending as well as ramping up of industrial defence production within Europe to ensure its own security as well of that of its allies, most urgently Ukraine, to be able to ensure Europe’s defence and Ukraine’s victory, even without US support, and to ensure that the European aid to Ukraine is structurally increased before President-Elect Trump’s term of office begins;

* an increase in financial support from the EU its Member States and other European states to multilateral institutions, to compensate for the likely decrease in funding to be received from the United States;

* the EU, in close consultation with its European allies, to assume global leadership and collaborate with other countries and international organisations to create a united front on various areas such as climate change while setting ambitious targets to encourage others to follow suit;

* Europe to take over a leading role and do more pioneering work in fighting climate change in order to achieve the global goals set by the Paris agreement also without institutional engagement from the United State;.

* the European Commission, in cooperation with the other European Partners and allies, to develop a comprehensive strategy to counter disinformation, both foreign and domestic, to avoid interference in the EU’s own democratic systems while promoting media literacy, critical thinking, and fact-based journalism

* The European Commission and EU diplomatic representatives to establish and maintain open channels of communication with U.S. sub-federal entities, such as states, municipalities, and cities, as well as other public diplomacy entities such as foundations, universities, NGO’s and civil society organisations, in order to continue collaborative efforts on key issues and shared goals, such as climate change, trade, and human rights, even if cooperation on a federal level declines under the Trump administration.

* The European Commission and EU diplomatic representatives to pursue new frameworks for cooperation with willing non-federal U.S. entities if they align with EU values.

* European companies to increase its efforts and investments into research and development of infrastructural resilience in areas such as the aerospace and telecommunications sector, thereby reducing its dependence on US scientific knowledge, technology and infrastructure.

* The EU must proactively strengthen its intelligence and cybersecurity infrastructure to safeguard against potential foreign interference and cyber threats, ensuring resilience in the face of diminishing external support from the USA.

* The Member States must not only cooperate with the US on the national level but

  on the regional and local level aswell in order to strenghten the bond between our nations, improve mutual understanding and create leverage for future cooperation on a national level.

* The EU and its Member States must work together with the US \- wherever it is possible \- regardless of who is sitting in the White House in order to tackle the common challenges we face.
