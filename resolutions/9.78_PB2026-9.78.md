*Noting that:*

* In the early morning hours of March 19th 2025, Istanbul’s ruling mayor and leading opposition politician Ekrem Imamoğlu was arrested on charges of corruption and terrorism, along with more than 80 of his close allies.

* The previous day, on March 18th 2025, Istanbul University had suddenly annulled Imamoğlu’s diploma, thereby effectively inhibiting him from contending presidential elections under Turkish law

* Following Imamoğlu’s arrest, Turkish authorities implemented several measures to restrict the public expression of dissent, including a prohibition of any public gatherings, the closure of public squares, the disruption of key transportation routes such as major roads and metro lines, the reduction of internet bandwidth, and restrictions of access to social media  
* Despite the ban on public gatherings, hundreds of thousands Turkish citizens took to the streets all over the country in the largest anti-government protests in over a decade, demanding Imamoğlu’s release and calling for the resignation of the government, including President Erdoğan  
* Turkish riot police brutally cracked down on the protests using tear gas and rubber bullets, and detained nearly 2000 protesters, most of whom were university students  
* Numerous journalists reporting about the protests have either been arrested or deported by Turkish authorities  
* On March 23rd, the same day that he was officially elected and nominated as presidential candidate by his party, a judge ordered Imamoğlu to be transferred to prison pending trial and he was dismissed as mayor of Istanbul

*Considering that:*

* The conspicuous timing of Imamoğlu’s arrest, just 4 days before he was due to be nominated as presidential candidate by the opposition party CHP, strongly suggests a political motive behind the charges against him

* Fifteen million Turkish citizens expressed their solidarity and support for Imamoğlu by voting for him in CHP's primary elections and nominating him as their presidential candidate  
* Imamoğlu is Türkiye’s leading opposition figure and is widely considered to be the only candidate capable of successfully challenging Erdoğan in presidential elections  
* Imamoğlu's arrest follows a common pattern of judicial harassment against opposition politicians, journalists, and civil society actors in Türkiye in recent years  
* The protests arose at a time of widespread dissatisfaction in Turkish society, driven by persistent inflation, soaring living costs, the erosion of democratic institutions, restricted press freedom, and the government’s failure to effectively address these issues

*Believing that:*

* The freedom from arbitrary detention, the freedom of the press, the freedom of association, and the right to fair trial are not only cherished liberal values, but also essential human rights

* Upholding the rule of law is a prerequisite for safeguarding human rights and political freedoms

* A strong and independent opposition is the backbone of any functioning democracy, and consequently, the arbitrary detention of opposition leaders constitutes a grave attack on democratic principles

* The Turkish citizens have the right to freely express their political will, protest peacefully, and participate in fair and transparent elections without fear of repression

*Notwithstanding that:*

Türkiye has proven to be a vital partner for the EU and its member states in areas such as NATO security cooperation, migration and refugee management, conflict resolution and mediation in Ukraine and the Middle East, as well as efforts to rebuild Syria, among other issues.  
LYMEC calls for:

* The Turkish government to uphold the rule of law, restore judicial independence, and respect the fundamental rights of opposition figures, journalists, and civil society actors

* The Turkish authorities to release Imamoğlu and his allies with immediate effect and grant them a fair trial under the rule of law

* A continued and escalated pressure on the EU and its member states to strongly condemn the recent developments in Türkiye and make clear that these oppressive measures are incompatible with democratic values and European partnership  
* A continued and escalated pressure on the EU and its members states to formally terminate Türkiye’s stalled EU accession procedure on the grounds of severe violations of democratic principles, human rights, and the rule of law, while explicitly leaving the door open for renewed accession negotiations in a post-Erdoğan era
