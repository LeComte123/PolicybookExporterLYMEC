(Passed Autumn 2024\)

Keywords: Ukraine, EU membership, gradual integration, observer status Recognising that:  
Russia's full-scale unprovoked aggression against Ukraine, as of 24 February, 2022, appeared to become a watershed pivotal point, after which the growing danger for the existence of liberal democracies can no longer be ignored;

Following the brutal invasion by the Russian Federation into Ukraine, the resilient Ukrainian people have not only defended their homeland but also safeguarded the values and democracy of the entirety of Europe;

Ukraine has made significant progress towards EU membership after obtaining candidate status on 17 June 2022, and maintains a high pace of reform towards EU convergence

Believing that:

The EU has an obligation to maintain a strong support for Ukraine as much as it takes for as long as needed;

Sending more military, humanitarian and economic aid is essential in supporting Ukraine;

Political support of Ukraine also is of the highest importance, such as promoting Ukraine’s rightful place within the European Union;

Ukraine can and must win the war, why the EU and its global allies need to provide sufficient and timely support for as long as it might take; to withstand Russian offensives, liberate its citizens and territories, deter the aggressor from new strikes, and punish all those guilty of crimes against humanity and law, to rebuild and revive the country;

LYMEC calls for:

Enhanced economic and trade relations between the EU and Ukraine, leading towards Ukraine’s full integration into EU’s Internal Market;

Allowing Ukraine to participate as full members in further EU cooperation areas, as soon as the corresponding negotiation chapter is closed;

Granting Ukraine observer status within the EU institutions, among other things by expanding the opportunities for officials from Ukraine to observe the work within EU institutions.

Renewing the basic principles of the EU Enlargement policy: Whilst ensuring strict compliance with criteria for EU accession, new frameworks of cooperation with EU candidate and EU neighbouring countries, in particular should be established as a matter of priority to guarantee a stable EU political and economic system and also connect third countries more closely with the EU to prevent other geopolitical forces from gaining too much influence, such as but not limited to Russia and China.
